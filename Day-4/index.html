CRICKET TEAM

VIRAT

Virat Kohli is an Indian cricketer and former captain of the Indian national team. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen in the history of cricket. Kohli has numerous records to his name, including being the fastest player to score 8,000, 9,000, 10,000, and 11,000 runs in One Day Internationals (ODIs). He has also been awarded the ICC Cricket World Cup Player of the Tournament and has received several prestigious awards for his contributions to cricket.

RAHUL

Rahul Dravid, often referred to as "The Wall," is a former Indian cricketer and captain of the Indian national team. He is known for his exceptional technique, patience, and ability to play long innings. Dravid has scored over 13,000 runs in Test cricket and is regarded as one of the greatest batsmen in the history of the game. He has also been a mentor and coach for the Indian cricket team, contributing to the development of young players.

RISHABH

Rishabh Pant is an Indian cricketer known for his aggressive batting style and wicket-keeping skills. He has represented India in all formats of the game and has played crucial innings in both Test and limited-overs cricket. Pant is known for his fearless approach to batting, often taking on bowlers with innovative shots. He has been a key player for the Indian team in various international tournaments and has earned a reputation as one of the most exciting young talents in world cricket.
